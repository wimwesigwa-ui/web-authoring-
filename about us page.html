BACKGROUND

Founded in 2012, Jumia Shopping Centre has grown into Africa’s leading e-commerce ecosystem. Our marketplace connects over 100,000 active sellers with tens of millions of consumers across the continent, offering a comprehensive selection of products ranging from electronics and everyday consumer goods to high-fashion apparel and local artisanal products.

Supported by Jumia Logistics—a tech-enabled logistics network enabling seamless deliveries to remote and urban areas—and JumiaPay, an integrated digital payment solution, Jumia creates a safe, reliable, and convenient online shopping experience designed to empower local entrepreneurship and transform daily life across Africa.
